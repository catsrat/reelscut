FAQ

Questions, answered.

What counts as a minute?

The length of the video you upload. A 20-minute podcast uses 20 minutes, no matter how many reels come out of it. Minutes reset at the start of every month.

Which languages does it support?

English, Hindi, Telugu and Tamil — captions are written in the language that's spoken.

Can I use a Google Drive link instead of uploading?

Yes. Share the file as “Anyone with the link” and paste the link into the app.

How do I cancel?

Plans are monthly subscriptions through Whop. Cancel anytime from your Whop account — you'll go back to the free minutes when the period ends.

Do I need editing skills or software?

No. Everything runs in your browser: upload, pick a few options if you like, and download finished reels.
